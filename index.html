Pillar 03 · The Coveted Body

Health is the only compounding asset.

An executive longevity program. Built around the labs you can only get in Switzerland and the habits you can only get from doing them.

The Verbier Protocol

What I do — and what I learned does the work.

A six-month protocol I run twice a year, with a clinic in Switzerland and a coach in Brooklyn. The full panel — GI, blood, hormone, iron, blood pressure, cardiopulmonary — twice annually. Nothing dramatic. The boring things, done.

Read the full protocol →

01 · Quarterly

Full blood & iron panel

The basic 60-marker panel I ignored for ten years. Done at the Clinique La Prairie standard, twice a year.

02 · Monthly

Three silent mornings

No call, no inbox before 11. Walking, reading, water. Earned its keep within six weeks.

03 · Weekly

Two strength sessions

Heavy and slow. Squats, deadlifts, presses, hangs. Boring on purpose. Tracked.

04 · Daily

Forty minutes of walking

The single most under-prescribed intervention for women in our forties. Outside, weighted, before noon.

05 · Daily

Eight hours protected

No meetings before 9. Lights down by 10. Phone in the kitchen. Not negotiable now.

06 · Annual

One true retreat

One week at a real clinic, one week with no plan. Different weeks. Both required.
